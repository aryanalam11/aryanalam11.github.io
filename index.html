About Me

Hi, I'm Aryan Alam, a first-year student at NJIT. This semester I'm taking IT 101, where I'm learning how websites are built using HTML and CSS. I'm interested in technology and in finding practical ways to use it to solve everyday problems.

Outside of class I stay busy. I work as a sales associate at a local grocery store, and I referee youth basketball games, where I was once named Referee of the Week. In high school I served as secretary of my 4-H student council, helping organize county fairs and presenting at the state level. I also co-founded a car detailing business, where I wash cars and handle the technology side of the business.
