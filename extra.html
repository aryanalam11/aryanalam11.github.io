Web Design Do's and Don'ts

After researching HTML elements and web design, here are the practices I learned to follow and to avoid when building a webpage.

5 Things You Should Do

  • Give every page a clear, descriptive <title>.
  • Add alt text to every image so it is accessible.
  • Use headings (<h1>, <h2>) in a logical order to organize content.
  • Keep text readable with a comfortable size and strong color contrast.
  • Use descriptive link text and test every link to make sure it works.

5 Things You Should Avoid

  • Autoplaying music or video that the visitor cannot control.
  • Cluttered layouts with too many fonts, colors, and animations.
  • Tiny or low-contrast text that is hard to read.
  • Broken links and missing images.
  • Pop-ups and ads that block the content.
