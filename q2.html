Why Arngren.net Is a Bad Website

The site I chose is Arngren.net, a Norwegian electronics and gadget retailer. It appears on the Ranking By SEO list of bad websites that was updated in 2025, and it is still cited as an example of poor web design. Here are 10 reasons it is a bad website:

  1. Information overload. The home page crams hundreds of products, prices, and links onto one page, so nothing stands out.
  2. No visual hierarchy. A visitor has no clear place to start looking, because every item competes for attention equally.
  3. Tiny text. Much of the writing is so small that it is hard to read without zooming in.
  4. Garish colors. Loud, clashing colors and heavy highlighting make the page tiring to look at.
  5. Low-quality images. Product photos are small, crowded together, and often poorly sized.
  6. Weak navigation. There is no clear menu or obvious way to browse by category, so finding a specific item takes a lot of scrolling.
  7. Not mobile friendly. The collage layout does not adapt to a phone screen, so users must pinch and zoom.
  8. Looks like a printed catalog. It feels like a jumble-sale flyer rather than a modern online store.
  9. Hurts trust. The cluttered, outdated look makes it hard to feel safe entering payment information.
  10. Unclear journey. Nothing guides a visitor toward a next step, such as a featured item, a search box, or a checkout path.
